# Bioinformatics Content — Overview

Five lectures (plus one self-study background page) covering the biological and
data science foundations of the Master's Programme in Bioinformatics, led by
**Nathaniel Street** (nathaniel.street@umu.se, Department of Plant Physiology,
Umeå University).

**Course:** 5BI00A Computing for Data-Driven Biology · 31 August – 29 September 2026

---

## Pre-HPC2N lectures

| # | Title | Description |
|---|-------|-------------|
| [00](../00.programme-introduction/programme-introduction/) | Programme Introduction | Programme overview, teaching philosophy, two-year structure, career pathways |
| [01](../01.what-is-bioinformatics/what-is-bioinformatics/) | What is Bioinformatics? | Origins, interdisciplinarity, epistemic limitations, AI tools, programme ILOs |

These two sessions are delivered before the HPC2N computing environment block.

## Post-HPC2N lectures

These sessions follow the HPC2N block (sections 1–10) and assume you can use the Linux command line, Git, and Slurm.

| # | Title | Key topics | ILOs |
|---|-------|-----------|------|
| [11](11.database-landscape/database-landscape.md) | Biological Database Landscape | INSDC, UniProt, GO, functional and specialist databases | 1, 4, 5, 7, 12 |
| [13](13.file-formats/file-formats.md) | Bioinformatics File Formats | FASTA, FASTQ, SAM/BAM, VCF, GFF/GTF, BED | 1, 4, 7, 9 |
| [14](14.fair-open-science/fair-open-science.md) | FAIR and Open Science | FAIR principles, reproducibility, open science | 3, 13 |
| [15](15.fair-in-practice/fair-in-practice.md) | FAIR in Practice | Three ways to run BLAST, PlantGenIE API, GEO metadata, essay briefing | 1, 3, 7, 9, 12, 13 |

**Background/self-study (not a taught session):** [BLAST and Sequence Similarity](12.blast/blast.md) — homology, E-values, BLOSUM, and how BLAST works conceptually. Read before Lecture 15, where the hands-on BLAST exercises (website, CLI, API) are taught.
